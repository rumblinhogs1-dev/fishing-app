Fishing Tips for Lake Fork

Target largemouth bass near submerged timber and creek channels. Use Texas-rigged soft plastics in natural colors. February is pre-spawn season — fish are moving shallow. Fishing license required; check TPWD for slot limits (16-24" must be released).
