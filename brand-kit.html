Logo Usage Guidelines

Minimum Size
App icon: 48px
Full logo: 120px wide
Clear Space
Maintain padding equal to the height of the "C" in CATCH around all sides
Backgrounds
Use on dark green (#1B4332) or dark backgrounds only. Never place on light backgrounds without the dark container.
Don't
Stretch, rotate, recolor, add drop shadows, or place on busy backgrounds
