7. Migration Notes

Current state: 94 raw color variables in variables.css. 29 are used in only 1 file.

Target: Components use --token-* semantic names. Raw --color-* vars remain for backwards compatibility until fully migrated.

How to migrate: Import design-tokens.css after variables.css in main.jsx. When editing a component, replace var(--color-green-700) with var(--token-primary), etc. No big-bang rewrite needed.
